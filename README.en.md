## About

Hi, I'm **Zwart** — an independent maker who enjoys turning ideas and everyday problems into practical software.

I build **web and Android apps**, explore **AI agents and automation**, and share some of my experiments as open-source projects. I believe good software isn't just about features; it should also feel simple to use.
